Reclaim captured content nothing needs any more, without losing retained review context. After a refresh, a deletion, a failed capture, a source check that found a change, or a change that releases a pin, such as a discarded draft, the daemon removes, in the background, content no saved session retains: of a replaced snapshot it keeps only the files that notes, references, conversations (resolved ones included) and drafts still point at, and a draft keeps the whole snapshot it was begun on until it is sent or discarded. A deleted session's content stays while another session shares it, deletion stays retry-safe, and nothing a read or capture is using is removed. Reads of an earlier snapshot serve only those retained files and refuse others as `stale_revision`. Optional `GYST_SNAPSHOT_QUOTA` (no default, read when the daemon starts) bounds one snapshot's captured text: the files with reviewed text changes must fit or the capture fails with `source_unavailable` (`quota_exceeded`), and other text beyond it is never stored and is recorded as unavailable with reason `quota`. A capture that runs out of disk space reclaims and retries once, then fails with `storage_full`, leaving saved sessions untouched.
